# Research Summary

## Steps
1. Define the question and scope.
2. Gather 3+ independent sources; prefer primary sources and recent dates.
3. Note agreements and conflicts between sources.
4. Write: **TL;DR**, key findings, open questions, sources.

## Rules
- Cite every factual claim with a link.
- Separate facts from inference.
- Flag low-confidence or outdated information.
